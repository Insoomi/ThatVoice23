Who we are

Advocacy that starts with people.

THATVOICE is an advocacy-focused organisation committed to amplifying voices, advancing rights, and empowering young people and communities to participate meaningfully in social change.

Based in Kitgum Municipality, Northern Uganda, we aim to create platforms where people—particularly young people and underserved communities—can express themselves, understand their rights, develop leadership skills, and help build a more just, inclusive, and accountable society.
